## Destination

Stage 5, the final boss Stage, built from six tickets that can be worked **in parallel**. Each ticket owns its own files and codes against the **shared contract** below, not against another ticket's code.

## Story beat

Out of the Lord's Lair, the party crosses a drawbridge, kills the gate guards, passes two gates, and the view zooms out over a plaza. The Dark Lord steps onto a balcony, trades words with the knight, turns into a flying shadow creature and is killed. It is night: full moon, clouds, moonlight.

## Shared contract (everyone codes against this)

**Resolution.** The Stage runs at 320×180 until the plaza. In the plaza the canvas renders **960×540** (`ZOOM = 3`). Sprites keep their pixel size, so the world shows 3× as much. The HUD stays at 320×180 and is scaled ×3.

**World layout** (world x in px; ground is `GROUND_Y`; y is negative above the ground view):

| Section | x range |
|---------|---------|
| Approach (cave exit at the left edge) | 0 – 356 |
| Moat + drawbridge (deck = ground) | 356 – 712 |
| Gatehouse (two towers, walkway, gate 1 at x 752–800) | 712 – 840 |
| Inner court (full screen of castle background) | 840 – 1176 |
| Gate 2 (open when entered) | 1176 – 1216 |
| Plaza (zoomed-out view starts at x 1200) | 1216 – 2070 |
| Keep and balcony (top right, about x 2040, y −230) | 2070 – 2160 |

Ticket 001 puts these in `layout.js`. If it hasn't landed yet, use the numbers above and import from `layout.js` once it has.

**Events** (on the Stage's event bus):

| Event | Emitted by | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| `guards-down` | 003 | All 8 gate guards are dead |
| `gate1-open` | 001 | The portcullis finished lifting |
| `plaza-entered` | 001 | The party is past gate 2; gate 2 has shut |
| `zoom-done` | 004 | The view finished pulling back |
| `boss-appeared` | 005 | The Dark Lord is on the balcony |
| `boss-phase-2` | 005 | He has turned into the shadow creature |
| `boss-dead` | 005 | The fight is won |

**Damage.** Every purple pulse and arrow does normal damage like other attacks. Allies, abilities (1 Lightning, 2 Arrow Storm, 3 Blessed Touch), the bow, and the Potion of Rage all work as in Stage 4.

## Open defaults (change any by editing the ticket)

- Minion HP is the same as a Pterodactyl.
- Skeleton Archers die in 1 hit.
- The boss beam repeats about every 8 s.
- The horse and Potion of Rage carry over. The horse waits outside the plaza.
- A short victory banner shows when the boss dies, with no extra dialogue.

## Integration order

All tickets can be built at the same time. Merge 001 first, because it registers the Stage. Then merge 002–006 in any order.
