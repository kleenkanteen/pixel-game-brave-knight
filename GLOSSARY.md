# Glossary

**Stage** — One self-contained level of the game. The game will have six; Stage 1 is the fall graveyard-to-farmer level.

**Brave Knight** — The player character. Rises from his grave at the start of Stage 1 believing he died in the war.

**Knight Width** — The width of the Brave Knight's body. The unit for distances in the world (e.g. the wheat field is 10 Knight Widths long; prompts appear within 1 Knight Width).

**Dialogue** — Spoken lines shown as `Speaker: text`, revealed quickly one character at a time, left to right.

**Prompt** — A short hint shown above a character (e.g. "Press space to pick up") only while the Brave Knight is within 1 Knight Width of the thing it concerns.

**Interact** — The Space-key action: picking up an item or talking to someone, depending on what is near.

**Strike** — Swinging the held sword with a click.

**Bow** — Picked up from its table in Stage 2 with B. Q switches between the sword and the bow.

**Draw** — Aiming the bow: press anywhere, pull back (drag away from where the arrow should go) and let go. A dotted arc shows where the arrow will land. Arrows are unlimited and stay where they hit.

**Exit** — The right edge of a Stage. Near it "Move to next screen →" shows; walking on fades to the next Stage.

**Objective** — The current goal, announced as "Objective" with the goal beneath (Stage 1: "Explore"), shown for 5 seconds.

**Allies** — The three fellow adventurers fighting in Stage 3: the Archer (green hat and outfit), the Mage (light purple cloak, staff with a sparkling blue orb) and the Holy Knight (dark armour with gold trim, gold-rimmed shield). They hold their ground and never drop below 1 HP.

**Monsters** — Stage 3's enemies: three Zombies (lunge for 1 damage and bounce back), a Werewolf (claw swipe, then leaps back 3 Knight Widths) and a Dark Mage (every 5 s drops a fireball on a random hero). Each goes for the closest hero; any hit knocks it back.

**Health bar** — The HP (out of 100) shown above each hero, number stacked over the bar. Monsters show a small red bar once hurt. A Strike or an arrow does 10 damage (3 to fell a zombie); an ally hit does 3 (10 to fell a zombie). At 0 HP the Brave Knight falls ("You have fallen...again. Second time in your life.") and a Restart button starts the Stage over.

**Lord's Lair** — Stage 4: a torch-lit cave behind the "Beware of dog" sign. An open cavern (one screen) with seven monsters, a low tunnel, then the Hydra's chamber (two screens) with a Ledge.

**Cave monsters** — Stage 4's enemies, asleep until a hero comes near: Cave Crawlers (pounce for 1 damage), Rock Golems (slow, barely knocked back; fist slam for 2) and Pterodactyls (fly out of sword reach; every 5 s they strain for 2 s — a mark blinks on the floor — then drop a dropping for 6 damage).

**Ledge** — Optional higher ground in the Hydra's chamber, reached by a Ladder (Space at its foot or top). The allies climb after him, one at a time. The Hydra's Fire blast can't reach it.

**Hydra** — The Stage 4 boss. Spits a slow fireball at the Brave Knight (sometimes an ally) every 5–10 s (8 damage). Every 15 s it charges a Fire blast, exposing its chin; the chin is the only place it can be hurt (1 hit per arrow, 30 hits kill it).

**Fire blast** — The Hydra's charged attack: "Attack its mouth to prevent a fire blast!" — 5 arrows in its chin within 6 s stop it; otherwise a wall of fire sweeps the whole floor (20 damage to everyone on it).

**Potion of Rage** — Dropped by the Hydra; two doses. Stage 5 always starts him with two doses (also on a restart at the Plaza). Press 4: the Brave Knight's own hits do 5× damage for 10 s while he radiates red fire.

**Hurt flash** — The Brave Knight's sprite flashes red for a moment whenever something hits him.

**Warning vortex** — The spinning ring of fire the Dark Mage's fireball marks the ground with. It lands 3 seconds later; step out of it.

**Dark Lord's Castle** — Stage 5, at night under a full moon: the approach out of the Lord's Lair, the moat and its drawbridge, the gatehouse (gate 1), the inner court, gate 2, then the Plaza.

**Gate guards** — Stage 5's eight guards: three Skeleton Archers on top of the gatehouse (lobbed arrows, 4 damage; one hit of anything fells them) and three Reptilians (big spiked purple beasts drooling green goo: crouch with jaws agape, then lunge and bite for 6) with two Werewolves on the drawbridge. With all eight down, gate 1's portcullis opens.

**Plaza** — The Dark Lord's arena past gate 2, which shuts behind the party. The view zooms out 3× (the canvas renders 960×540; sprites keep their pixel size). Twenty-three floating rune-stone Platforms (two stairs, then zigzags to a high walkway and a staircase up to a perch three-quarters of the way up), a ritual circle, and the Balcony at the top right. The horse waits outside. Falling here restarts at the Plaza.

**Dark Lord** — The Stage 5 boss. Steps onto the Balcony, talks, then turns into a flying shadow creature at the top of the Plaza: 30 hits (an arrow is 1, a Lightning Strike 4; Blessed Touch doubles, and Rage doubles the Brave Knight's own arrows).

**Pulse beam** — The Dark Lord's attack, about every 8 s: a small purple glint shows his aim for 2 s, then a straight beam fires that way, burning everyone in its path (20 damage). Step out of line.

**Ultimate** — The Dark Lord's blast every 40 s: the whole Plaza, floor and Platforms alike, burns (50 damage) except one safe slice three Knight Widths wide, top to bottom. A 7 s warning first: the doomed area glows violet, the slice is lit gold with "SAFE" and a countdown, and the allies run into it.

**Shadows** — The Dark Lord's seven flying minions: all seven swoop in from the top, left or right at the start of the fight, 1.2 s apart (each comes once) and spits a purple pulse at a hero every 5–10 s (8 damage). A Pterodactyl's health.
